Descriptor-Group Comparison under a Revised Ontology, with Dimensionality Matching
=================================================================================
Spectral centroid is separated from width/shape descriptors: it is an intensity-weighted mean
wavelength, i.e. a band-POSITION statistic, and grouping it with shape overstated the case for
shape over position. Groups are compared both in full and at matched size (best single descriptor).

Features-Only Classifiers vs. the CNN, by Named Spectral Feature Group
=======================================================================
Stratified 5-fold CV, same dyes / photon cutoff (1000) / outlier filtering / per-dye sample sizes as the CNN validation.
Classifiers: linear discriminant (primary) and bagged trees (guards against understating a
feature group that is informative only nonlinearly). Both are reported; neither is tuned.

=== pairwise network: BODIPY 4/10 (n = 1200 molecules) ===
CNN reference: 5-fold CV accuracy 0.738 +/- 0.016 (from validation_results, same dyes and preprocessing)

  Peak position      1      0.588 +/- 0.026          0.578 +/- 0.012
  Shoulder           2      0.631 +/- 0.021          0.567 +/- 0.018
  Band shape         6      0.702 +/- 0.026          0.714 +/- 0.025
  Amplitude          1      0.578 +/- 0.016          0.530 +/- 0.027
  All 10 features    10     0.735 +/- 0.012          0.730 +/- 0.013

CNN minus all-10-features gap: +0.003 accuracy -- within about 2 CNN CV standard deviations, i.e. the named features account for most of what the CNN achieves.
